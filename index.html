Rookie Draft History

Every pick from past rookie drafts, graded with hindsight. Each class is re-ranked by what its players are worth today (the same values as the rest of the site), and each pick is compared to the slot it was made at. A 2.02 whose player re-ranks as the 4th-best in his class is a steal; a 1.03 who now ranks 19th is a bust.
